# v10.27.0 · Coach Walter and flexible fraction homework

In the eight-question decimal warm-up, one spot rotates between catching Walter’s comparison mistake, changing a single digit to make a new number, and ordinary practice. On a multiplication question, learners can predict the size of the answer and change their minds. On ribbon stories, they can cover the numbers or go straight to solving.

Walter offers fresh decimal comparisons on a later day. Parent settings can show when a learner chose a picture and later answered a different, comparable question independently. After two such pairs from different practice problems, the picture button comes first for that skill and difficulty. All help choices stay available.

Homework Check reads more ways of describing lengths cut from clothing, including common inch wording and separate sentences. For an unfamiliar fraction story, learners can keep the words on screen, type their own addition or subtraction, and choose an area model, number line or equivalent fractions.
