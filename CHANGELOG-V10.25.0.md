# v10.25.0 · Division and decimal tutoring

In whole-number division practice, Walter remembers multiplication the learner has checked. Reusing a group size that fits leads to subtraction. If those groups use too much, Walter keeps the current question open and points to the checked amounts. The learner can enter the whole answer or go back to their groups. Repeated batches share one line and only the current question is shown.

New eight-question decimal warm-ups let learners move a digit and predict its value, check the counting part of multiplication or division, and compare two ribbon stories with the same numbers. Walter keeps checked work in place while the learner fixes the next part. A later session can open with a fresh question at the practiced difficulty. Parents can see dated observations, the support used, and whether a later check was solved independently.

Earlier saved warm-ups keep their original questions.
