# Lily Math v10.9.0

November 9, 2026.

## Walter across the app

- Practice, walkthroughs, authored lessons, repairs, and checked homework surface one main help action next to the check or current work. More help keeps the different worked example and other ways to ask.
- After a missed check, Walter uses the current step, entered work, and any checked field or mistake pattern. If the learner is still stuck, Walter offers a smaller action or a model. Multiplication worksheet help follows the active box. Decimal grids retain their checked tools.
- Help preserves drafts and accepted work. A format issue stays separate from a missed math check, and helped work is recorded as supported.

## Unit 5

- Simplifying follows the common factor the learner chose. Walter distinguishes changing only one number from writing an equivalent fraction that can still be reduced.
- Estimation uses whole and half marks. Addition provides aligned strips for equivalent fractions, checked jumps on a number line, and cues about the common piece size and counts.
- Mixed-number help distinguishes full groups, leftover pieces, typing format, and piece size. On 3/2 and similar problems, the learner can mark one whole on the strip. Equivalent mixed numbers and the fraction glyphs supported by the app are accepted.
