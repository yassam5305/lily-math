# v10.22.0 · Build and connect fraction drawings

In Unit 5 area-model addition and matching area-model homework, **Drawing → Build it like class** opens blank bands. Shade the first amount in rows and the second in columns. Choose how many equal parts to make in the other direction. The cuts preserve the selected area. Each drawing can be edited separately. The regular calculation can be finished at any point.

After completing the calculation, **Why are these equal?** aligns smaller boxes with strip pieces. For 1/2 + 3/8, fourteen shaded sixteenths fill seven pairs. **Try a half with thirds** offers a separate, ungraded problem. Add the missing cuts and enter a sum. A half with fifths is offered when the original addends are already a half and a third.

Walter can recognize a plausible group size entered as a numerator. After the learner checks 14/16, an entry of 2 or 2/8 prompts a question about how many groups of two fit in fourteen. The learner supplies the count. Choosing an equivalent fraction such as 14/16 or 7/8 still completes the original addition.

Drafts and cuts stay with the problem. Guided support counts as help, while the optional follow-up has its own answer and feedback. All three activities are optional.
