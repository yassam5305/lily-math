# v10.23.0 · Decimal warm-up with Walter

Parents can enable a three-question decimal warm-up for an individual player in Practice settings and privacy. Home offers the warm-up alongside the regular class and homework options. A session connects a familiar fact or decimal with place value and a short application. Topics include decimal multiplication and division, powers of ten, comparing, rounding, addition and subtraction.

Walter offers a starting clue, a picture and a separate worked example. Two whole-square drawings show how the same digit represents different amounts in adjacent places. After a learner checks a whole-number fact, they can reuse it to solve the related decimal problem. Each player keeps their own draft and recent warm-up observations on the device. A later session can revisit an application that needed support. Regular lesson levels and scores are tracked separately.
