# Lily Math v10.16.0

November 11, 2026.

## Remaining v11 prototype features

- Unit 5 now has a visible shortcut near the top of Home and an exposed ten-lesson card. Start, saved work, and due check-in actions use the existing lesson state; no Browse expansion is needed.
- New Unit 4 Lesson 9 application problems distinguish completely filled containers from enough containers to hold everything. The learner chooses trial counts, checks multiplication and the remainder, and makes the final interpretation. Correct oversized or undersized trials remain exploration rather than arithmetic misses. A separate story entry is available under Browse all math.
- Saved scratch work now accompanies Unit 4 Lesson 9 as well as the ten authored Unit 5 lessons. Unit 4 Lesson 9 uses next-day follow-up after new or supported method practice, at the difficulty actually practiced. Earlier saved lesson generations retain their original problems.
- Dynamic fraction addition and subtraction methods offer Change piece count after a common denominator is checked. Valid changes reopen the dependent renaming/calculation, preserve the original problem and scratch work, and retain bounded historical evidence of replaced checks. Invalid choices, cancellation, or the same denominator leave accepted arithmetic intact. This works in the shared authored lessons, contextual stories, and exact fraction homework.
- Eligible lessons offer Try a whole problem my way before the worked example. Contextual tasks are preferred where their complete question and answer checker are available. The learner can ask for help understanding the question, drawing a model, or checking an approach, or open the existing checked steps. Answers, help, revisions, and prompted methods remain distinct.
- Completing one open problem does not claim completion of the full lesson or raise difficulty/readiness. It schedules a fresh method opportunity at the practiced difficulty, preserving an earlier pending future check and its difficulty together.

## Includes the previously local v10.15.0 work

- Concrete clarification and changed teaching actions after ineffective help.
- Unit 5 Math model / My scratch paper workspace modes.
- Progressive different-problem examples that return to the unfinished entry.
- Fresh whole-answer checks separate from checked-method evidence.
- Conservative records of helpful and unsuccessful approaches.

## Coverage and limits

The existing 29 practice families, 106 authored lesson routes, and 133 calendar entries remain distinct. This release extends existing routes rather than claiming the 27 mapped calendar entries are authored lessons. Unit 5 Lesson 11 remains mapped review. At sampled difficulties 1–3, 59 authored routes offer initial open-problem practice and support delayed whole-problem tasks (the latter still require prior evidence); 47 retain their checked flows. Open-problem mode is offered only for supported complete questions with answer checkers; it does not grade the chosen approach, freehand drawing, or spoken/written reasoning. Static ordinary practice cards retain their existing denominator flow. Prototype-only backup history is not promoted into main-app learning evidence.

Physical iPad, Safari, and Pencil behavior and actual learning gains have not been established. Tests use disposable synthetic learners. Prior releases and the local v11 prototypes are preserved. Publication verification is recorded separately.
