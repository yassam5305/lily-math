# Lily Math v10.11.0

November 11, 2026.

- Restores an always-visible blocks diagram beneath the active Unit 5 work, alongside the division layout. The original amount is shaded; a separate blank model lets the learner tap to shade or erase their own blocks.
- Extends work beside the original problem to mixed-number conversion, fraction benchmarks, common denominators, equivalent fractions, fraction addition, number-line jumps and comparison. The original fractions and already-checked work stay visible beside the answer fields.
- Covers authored Unit 5 lessons 1–10 and the corresponding practice walkthroughs; Lesson 11 continues to select those review activities. Existing Check and Help me controls and targeted Walter responses remain available.
- Drawing never fills in an answer, submits a check, or counts as a mistake or delivered tutor help. Target models start blank. Authored lesson drawings and typed drafts survive pausing and reopening; practice drawings remain available across steps of the current walkthrough.
- Keeps both fraction drawings when progressing from equivalent fractions to their sum. Fresh problems start with fresh blank drawings.
- Preserves the original fraction exactly in diagram text and accessibility labels: 3/2 stays 3/2 while the learner works out its mixed-number form.

Tested with synthetic beginner cases, including suggestions from local Qwen, at phone and tablet widths. No learner data was sent to Qwen. No progress-schema change; previous releases and separate local v11 prototypes are preserved.
